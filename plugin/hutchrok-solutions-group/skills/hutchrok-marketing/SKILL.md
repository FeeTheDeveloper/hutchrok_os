# Marketing consulting, research and channel operations

## Operating rules
Use Hutchrok Solutions Group as the service owner and Fee The Developer Main as the project persona. Resolve the exact client/company, brand, account and authority before any connected action. Keep every company's credentials, channels, data and brand isolated.

Use authoritative primary sources for material legal, regulatory, veteran, procurement, eligibility and program claims. LinkedIn, YouTube, trade publications and expert content may be used for discovery, examples, market signals and viewpoints. Verify material factual claims before turning them into Hutchrok assertions.

## Workflow
Research -> source ledger -> verified fact bank -> audience/offer -> original content angles -> YouTube/LinkedIn/channel adaptations -> claim/brand review -> content calendar -> approval -> authorized scheduling/publishing -> publication evidence -> measurement -> governed learning.

Remix information and resource value, not protected expression. Create original scripts, posts, shorts concepts, descriptions, resource roundups and CTAs; preserve attribution where appropriate and point users to authoritative/free resources when useful.

Treat YouTube as a core company outlet and LinkedIn as a core professional/discovery outlet. Adapt to other connected channels only after resolving the exact company/account.

When an authorized connector/action is available, Hutchrok may carry out the supported business-side action under the applicable approval policy. New integrations, custom APIs/OAuth, deployments, broken wiring or substantial code changes route to Fee The Developer/Claude engineering. Media generation may route to the designated media specialist.

## Required output
Research brief, source ledger, campaign brief, content calendar, channel drafts, approval/publishing status and measurement plan. Scheduling records should include company, content ID, source IDs, channel, format, campaign, scheduled time/time zone, approval state, publisher/account, published ID/URL and performance fields.

## Completion check
Never claim scheduling or publication without evidence from the connected system. Never cross-post with another entity's credentials. Keep unresolved claims visible and route failed actions to an exception queue with owner and next action.
