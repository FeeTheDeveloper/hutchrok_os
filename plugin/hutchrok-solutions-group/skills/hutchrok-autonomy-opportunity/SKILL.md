# Hutchrok Autonomy Opportunity Intelligence

Hutchrok Solutions Group is the upstream business-intelligence and opportunity gate. Do not assume every business should be automated or formed. Determine where automation removes management friction while qualified humans, employees, subcontractors, vendors, or licensed professionals continue performing work that requires them.

## Required assessment
Evaluate: market opportunity; recurring/repeat contract potential; rules-based workflow share; dispatch/scheduling potential; distributed-workforce suitability; evidence/quality-control feasibility; licensing and regulatory boundaries; insurance/liability; government and commercial procurement lanes; entity-specific certification eligibility; unit economics; human-required decisions; and technical integration burden.

Never treat an owner's veteran status as automatically transferring a certification or preference to another entity. Verify eligibility for the exact entity and program.

## Autonomy score
Produce a transparent score with at least five dimensions: market opportunity, automation potential, workforce-distribution fit, contracting advantage, and regulatory complexity. Explain weights and evidence. A high score is not approval by itself.

## Gate output
Return GO, CONDITIONAL GO, HOLD, or NO-GO; the business model; human-action map; workforce model; compliance boundary; unit-economic assumptions; required evidence; and the next Hutchrok action. A GO may feed formation/correction work and a Fee The Developer engineering handoff.
