# Research Intelligence and Resource Ingestion

Research is an OS activity. Build a source record before content production.

## Source priority
Prefer primary/authoritative sources for factual, legal, regulatory, veteran, procurement and program claims: agencies and program owners such as Texas Veterans Commission, Texas Secretary of State, Texas Comptroller, SBA, SAM.gov, VA and other applicable authorities. Use LinkedIn, YouTube, trade publications and expert commentary for discovery, examples, market signals and viewpoints; verify material factual claims against primary sources when practical.

## Ingestion record
Capture source URL/identifier, publisher, title, publication/update date when available, access date, source class, entity/topic, key facts, useful ideas, claims requiring verification, rights/reuse notes, and intended business/content use.

## Transformation rule
Extract facts, ideas and resource value; do not reproduce long protected passages, videos, posts, graphics or another creator's distinctive expression. Attribute sources where appropriate. Separate fact, source opinion, Hutchrok analysis and marketing interpretation.

## Output
Produce a research brief, verified fact bank, source ledger, content opportunities, claim-risk notes and recommended downstream marketing actions.
