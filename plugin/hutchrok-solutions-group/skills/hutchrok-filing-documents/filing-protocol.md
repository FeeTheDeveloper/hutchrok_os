# Filing creation and editing protocol

## Intake and evidence
Record client ID, exact entity name, state, legal type, filing action, existing state file number if any, source document version, owners, governing persons, organizer, agent consent, addresses, purpose, effective date and attachments. Do not collect unnecessary personal identifiers. Use a field table with proposed value, evidence reference, verification status and unresolved question.

## Document actions
- New formation: retrieve the correct official form and current instructions. Use Texas Form 205 only for the applicable Texas LLC case; corporations and other jurisdictions require their own forms.
- Draft edit: preserve the original; produce versioned working and review copies and a field-level before/after table.
- Signed but unfiled document: retain signed original unchanged; produce a new unsigned draft for renewed execution if terms change.
- Accepted filing: never rewrite the accepted instrument as if the agency approved new facts. Determine the appropriate amendment or correction process, obtain current form/instructions and build a separate packet.
- Termination, reinstatement, conversion or merger: research transaction-specific approvals, tax clearance and attachments. Identify professional-review issues and never use a generic formation form.

## Quality gate
Check all required fields, cross-document consistency, ownership sums when collected, signatory capacity, agent consent, dates, selected alternatives, continuation pages, fee calculation and waiver evidence. Render generated PDF/DOCX files and inspect every page for clipping and missing text. When editing an official PDF, preserve its form layout and office-use areas. Label a nonofficial working document as a draft; do not portray a homemade template as the agency form.

## Delivery and evidence
Deliver an editable source where feasible, unsigned review PDF, field map, change log, missing-items list and operator checklist. Never manufacture a signature or notarization. Capture client approval against exact version, operator review, actual submission receipt and agency disposition separately. Keep submission and acceptance distinct. No actual submission is authorized by this skill package itself.

## Professional boundaries
Support document preparation from verified instructions. Flag customized legal terms or tax elections for appropriately qualified review. Do not promise approval, asset protection or a legal outcome. Continue useful drafting rather than blocking the entire case on a question that affects only one field.
