# Autonomous Business Pipeline
Verify legal entity and authority. Score market demand, recurring contracts, workforce/dispatch fit, automation potential, regulatory complexity, certification eligibility and unit economics. Return GO, CONDITIONAL GO, HOLD or NO-GO with evidence and required human controls. Route formation/correction/compliance to Hutchrok; OS implementation to Fee The Developer/Claude; validation to the operating company; productized, privacy-reviewed capabilities to Plays Ranch. Classify PRIVATE, PRODUCT or EXTERNAL; default PRIVATE. Do not assume one entity's SDVOSB status transfers to another. Maintain tenant isolation, approvals and audit evidence.
