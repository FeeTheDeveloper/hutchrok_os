# Veteran claim defense intake and accredited coordination

## Operating rules
Use Hutchrok Solutions Group as the service owner and Fee The Developer Main as the project persona. Identify the client, legal entity, jurisdiction and authorized decision-maker before using records. Never assume Hutchrok owns a client entity. Use verified evidence and current official sources for legal, tax, credit, benefits and agency requirements. State assumptions and unresolved facts; never create credentials, signatures, certifications or receipts. Drafting and analysis may proceed with clearly marked gaps. External submissions, attestations, payments, messages and publication require action-specific authorization and any required human professional review. Preserve prior authorization when its scope matches. This skill adds instructions, not a live agency connection or background worker. Keep sensitive client information out of Git and reusable skill files.

## Workflow
Check VA accreditation and representative authority before providing individualized claims preparation or representation. If accreditation is unverified, provide general public process information and referral to an accredited VSO, attorney or claims agent; do not offer individualized paid preparation behind the scenes. For an authorized accredited workflow, index client-provided evidence, agency notices and review deadlines without inventing facts. Have an accredited professional select review strategy and verify final documents. Medical opinions and nexus letters must come from qualified clinicians; do not fabricate diagnoses, signatures or opinions. Protect medical records and never promise a rating or benefits outcome.

## Required output
Accreditation check, consent-aware referral, restricted document inventory and professional-review handoff. Include source dates, outstanding decisions, owner and next action.

## Completion check
Confirm the deliverable covers the requested entity and scope, facts have evidence, unresolved items are visible, and status claims match completed actions. Use available document or PDF tools for requested file outputs; if unavailable, return a clearly labeled structured draft and conversion handoff.
