# Site audit and source register
Reviewed 2026-09-30. Coverage basis: publicly retrievable pages; not an authenticated portal or exhaustive private-service inventory. Skills are operating procedures authored for the owner, not statements that services are activated.

## Findings requiring decisions
1. The homepage/free-filing/launch material presents an independent no-purchase filing offer; membership presents subscription-gated filing. Obtain the owner's current policy and reconcile website copy before making a binding quote.
2. Homepage emphasizes Texas LLCs while services includes corporations and broader structuring. Default delivery to Texas; research other jurisdictions only when requested, and confirm commercial availability before promising service.
3. State requirements take precedence over site guides. Revalidate waiver eligibility, submission channel and necessary attachments. Do not hard-code one-free-filing as a statutory restriction or guarantee a waiver from one owner's status.
4. Registered-agent activation depends on availability and documented consent. No client may be assigned Hutchrok's address automatically.
5. SBA certification and SAM identifiers shown by the company need official verification before use in bids. No independent certification verification was completed in this audit.
6. Veteran claims services need verified accredited-professional scope; website language alone establishes neither accreditation nor permission to represent.
7. No customer login, state filing, payment, live monitoring, agency integration or repository connection was activated by creating these skills.

## Observed commercial reference, not an approved quotation
Membership page: Operator $59/month, Growth $129/month, Command $249/month; stated yearly filing allowances 1/2/4 and subscriber discounts 10/15/20 percent. Internal preparation targets stated as 7–10, 3–5 and 1–2 business days after intake, not agency promises.
Launch page starting prices: website $300; brand identity $180; logo $90; business email $60; domain/hosting $75; compliance/operations $135. Verify scope, renewal costs and current price before quoting.
