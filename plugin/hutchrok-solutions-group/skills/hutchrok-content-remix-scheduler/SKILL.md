# Research-to-Marketing Content Engine

## Pipeline
Research brief -> verified fact bank -> audience/offer -> original content angles -> channel adaptations -> brand/legal review -> schedule -> approval -> publish through authorized connection -> capture evidence -> measure -> learn.

## Channels
Treat YouTube as a core business outlet and LinkedIn as a core professional/discovery outlet. Adapt to other connected company channels only when the exact company, brand, account and authority are resolved.

## Remix standard
Remix the information, resource and insight, not copied expression. Create original scripts, posts, shorts concepts, carousels, descriptions, CTAs and resource roundups. Preserve attribution where needed and link users to authoritative/free resources when that is the value proposition.

## Scheduling
Maintain a per-company content calendar with content ID, source IDs, channel, format, target audience, campaign, draft status, approval status, scheduled time/time zone, publisher identity, published URL/ID, and performance fields. Scheduling/publishing is an OS action and must use an actually connected authorized channel; never claim access that is absent.

## Escalation
Use Hutchrok/plugin capabilities for research, planning, drafting, scheduling and supported actions. Escalate new connectors, broken integrations, custom APIs, deployment, or substantial system wiring to Fee The Developer/Claude engineering with a structured handoff. Media generation may route to the designated media specialist.

## Guardrails
Never cross-post using the wrong company's credentials or brand. Never turn unverified eligibility/program claims into marketing promises. Preserve platform rules, consent requirements and copyright/attribution boundaries.
