# Federal funding concept notes

## NSF PESOSE 26-506

**Decision:** Conditional go for eligibility/evidence work; no application yet.  
**Deadline:** March 2, 2027, 5:00 PM submitter local time.  
**Potential lane:** Track 1, up to $300,000, if eligibility and fit are confirmed.

### Working concept

Research and community infrastructure for a secure, auditable open-source ecosystem supporting tenant-isolated AI-agent governance. Candidate research areas include capability declarations, deny-by-default authorization, approval receipts, connector isolation, deterministic audit records, and recovery/idempotency patterns.

### Evidence needed

- A research contribution, not merely product development or consulting.
- A public open-source code and licensing plan.
- Community need, external contributors/users, governance, maintenance, and security-response plans.
- A U.S.-resident employee PI meeting solicitation requirements.
- Organizational eligibility and all required registrations.
- Clear public benefit and metrics for security, sustainability, and adoption.

### Stop rules

- Do not describe the current private plugin as an established open-source ecosystem.
- Do not claim community adoption, research provenance, or external users without records.
- Do not publish proprietary code or secrets merely to manufacture eligibility.

## NIH/CDC/FDA Parent SBIR PA-27-100

**Decision:** Hold.  
**Next standard deadline:** January 5, 2027, 5:00 PM local time.

Required before concept drafting: a specific biomedical/public-health R&D problem; participating institute/topic fit; scientific lead/PI; hypothesis and aims; human-subjects/data plan where relevant; technical feasibility evidence; commercialization path; and registrations. General AI governance or business automation is not enough.

## NIDILRR SBIR Phase I forecast

**Decision:** Monitor and narrow a real concept.  
**Forecast:** HHS-2027-ACL-NIDILRR-BISA-0308; estimated deadline December 16, 2026; anticipated Phase I awards of roughly $95,000-$100,000.

Candidate concept must directly improve disability, independent living, or rehabilitation outcomes. Keep accessibility, user research with people with disabilities, measurable functional benefit, privacy, and feasibility central. Wait for the final NOFO/topics before treating it as open.

## BJA National Center for Veterans Justice

**Decision:** No-go as sole applicant; consortium only.  
**Deadline:** October 30, 2026.

A credible lead needs national justice-system, clinical/treatment, veteran-culture, training, technical-assistance, evaluation, privacy, and grant-management capability. Hutchrok may consider a bounded technology/program-management role only if a qualified lead owns the substantive program and deadlines allow responsible diligence.
