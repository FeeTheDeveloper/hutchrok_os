## Human submission gate

The packet may be marked `READY_FOR_HUMAN_SUBMISSION` only when every material field is resolved, the clean unsigned copy matches the approved draft, and a reviewer has approved the exact packet digest. A human operator must separately handle signature, attestation, checkout, payment, and final submission.
