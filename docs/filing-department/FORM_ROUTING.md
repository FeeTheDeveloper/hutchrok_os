# Texas SOS Correction Form Routing

Use this guide only after the source instrument and requested result are known. Record the route and the official instruction page in the case file. If the facts do not fit a route cleanly, stop and obtain qualified legal or tax review.

## Route table

| Situation | Candidate route | Required checks before drafting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An unsubmitted SOSPortal draft contains a data-entry error | Edit the existing draft | Verify displayed entity, draft type, current values, and draft expiration. Save and preview only. |
| SOS rejected a submission and exposes an edit or resubmit workflow | Correct the rejected submission | Preserve the rejection reasons; map every reason to a draft change and evidence item; verify the portal deadline. |
| An accepted filing was inaccurate when originally filed, contains an erroneous statement, or was defectively executed | Form 403 Certificate of Correction may apply | Identify the exact original instrument and filing date; confirm the correction is within the legal scope of a correction; identify authorized signer. |
| The registered agent or registered office is the only later business change | Form 401 Statement of Change may apply | Verify current agent and office; obtain agent consent; confirm the office is a Texas street address where service can occur during business hours. |
| The entity is making a later change to its certificate of formation | Form 424 Certificate of Amendment may apply | Verify entity-specific approval and signer requirements; identify every provision added, altered, or deleted. |
| The requested change concerns a Comptroller PIR, OIR, franchise-tax account, or veteran-owned status | Comptroller handoff | Do not route a Comptroller report correction through an SOS registered-agent filing. Apply `../TEXAS_COMPTROLLER_HANDOFF.md`. |

## Official constraints checked 2026-10-07

- Texas SOS lists Forms 401, 403, and 424 under amendments and corrections. Its forms index says not to attach a PDF form when submitting through SOSPortal; the online screens collect the required information.
- Form 401 is intended when the only change is the registered agent or registered office. Registered-agent consent is required, and the registered office cannot be solely a mailbox or telephone answering service.
- Form 403 corrects an accepted filing that was inaccurate, erroneous, or defectively executed. It is not a general replacement for a later change in business facts.
- Form 424 handles amendments to the certificate of formation and has entity-specific approval and signer rules.
- SOS instructions warn against including personal identifying information in a filing. Filed documents are public records.
